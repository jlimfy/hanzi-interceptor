Rules of engagement
  • Three shields. A wrong meaning shot costs one.
  • Every meaning falls in its own lane, so the right one is never hidden behind a wrong one — line up under it and fire.
  • Wrong meanings can be ignored entirely; let them drift off the bottom. Only letting the correct one fall past costs a shield.
  • Answer quickly for a speed bonus; three in a row starts a multiplier, up to ×5.
  • Every correct answer can drop an upgrade pod. Fly into it before it falls away.
  • The drift speeds up every four sentences.
